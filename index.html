How it works

Optimistic
Takes the better reading from each weather model. Good when you're flexible and can turn back if needed.
Balanced
Averages the two models. A reasonable middle ground for day trips.
Pessimistic
Takes the worse reading. Best when you're committing to a long drive or multi-day trip.
Time
When the weather data was last fetched. Goes amber if over an hour old.
↻ Refresh
Forces fresh data from all weather sources, ignoring the cache.
